# Stein Trademark Policy

Stein's code is open source. Its **name and logo are not**. This page explains how you can use
them. In short: share Stein freely, talk about it freely, but don't put the Stein name on a version
you changed.

The Stein marks are owned by Breno Xavier ([github.com/brenoxavier](https://github.com/brenoxavier)).
They include the names "Stein", "Stein Loader", "Stein Bridge", "Stein Client", "Stein GFX",
"Stein SDK" and "Stein Bot", and the Stein logo and icons.

The code licences (MPL-2.0 and MIT) do not grant any rights to these marks (MPL-2.0, section 2.3).

## Allowed without asking

- **Redistributing unmodified Stein** under its own name and logo. For example: listing it on a mod
  platform such as Modrinth, including it in a modpack, or shipping it inside a launcher, as long as
  the files are the official, unchanged releases.
- **Talking about Stein**: articles, videos, reviews, tutorials, and screenshots.
- **Describing your own work truthfully**: "a mod for Stein Loader", "compatible with Stein",
  "made with the Stein SDK", "based on Stein".

## Not allowed

- Distributing a **modified** version of Stein under the Stein name, logo or icons. This includes
  a fork, a repackaged build, or the official build with files added or removed.
- Using the marks in a way that suggests your project is official, endorsed by Stein or made by
  its author when it isn't.
- Using the marks in the name of your own product, or a confusingly similar name or logo.

## If you fork Stein

You are welcome to. The MPL-2.0 lets you change and distribute the code. Your fork must:

1. **Use a different name and logo.** Remove the Stein name, logo and icons from everything users
   see: window titles, menus, installers, the launcher, and download pages.
2. **Credit Stein**, visibly, with a link back. For example, in your README and in your
   about/credits screen: "Based on Stein by Breno Xavier — https://github.com/brenoxavier".
3. **Keep the licence and copyright notices** required by the MPL-2.0.

Internal identifiers such as package names (`dev.xavier.stein.*`), mod ids, config file names and
system properties may stay as they are. Users don't see them, and renaming them would break
compatibility.

## Everything else: ask

If you want to use the marks in a way not covered here, open an issue or contact
[@brenoxavier](https://github.com/brenoxavier) on GitHub. One example is shipping a modified or
integrated Stein under its name inside another client or launcher. Permission is given in writing,
case by case.
